Privacy

Last updated 17 November 2026

Guess the Ask is a daily house-price guessing game. There are no accounts and no ads. This page says what we collect and why.

What we collect when you play

  • Your guesses, scores and how long each round took, along with the house you were guessing.
  • Your device and browser type, the page you came from, and your approximate location (town or city level), worked out from your IP address.

This is anonymous. It isn't linked to your name, and we don't ask for one. We use it to run the game, fix problems, and understand how people guess house prices in different places. We may publish or share results in aggregate, such as "Boston guesses run low".

Storage on your device

Your game progress, streak and stats are kept on your own device so the game can remember you. Analytics doesn't set a tracking identifier on your device.

If you use a feature that asks for details

  • Daily reminder: if you enter your email, we store it only to send that reminder. You can ask us to delete it.
  • Leagues: the nickname you choose is shown to the other people in your league.

Who handles the data

Analytics is provided by PostHog, using its EU servers. The game is hosted on Netlify. We don't sell your personal data.

Your rights

You can ask what we hold about you, or ask for it to be deleted. Message us on Instagram at @guesstheask.
